## Slice 014-02 — accrual spacing rule lights real RAG

**Goal:** Turn the dense stream of session-stop captures into a well-formed
`progress(t)` series by applying a simple minimum-interval spacing/dedup rule at
capture time, so accrued (not just git-backfilled) history clears ADR-0012's
minimum-history gate and a worked project's card shows a **real** `on_track` /
`at_risk` RAG band derived from captured history.

**DoR:**
- ✅ Slice 014-01 landed: session-stop captures are accruing under `stateDir`.
- ✅ Probe-confirmed: `collectObservation` appends one record per call with no
  dedup (`src/state.mjs:245`), so an unbounded hook would bloat history — the
  rule this slice adds is genuinely needed, not speculative.
- ✅ `deriveForecast` already lights real RAG once a series clears the ADR-0012
  gate (`src/derive.mjs`) — this slice makes the **captured** series clear it,
  and verifies, rather than changing the derivation.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

1. **Minimum-interval spacing at capture.** A capture is skipped (or coalesced)
   when the most recent existing observation for that project is newer than a
   documented minimum interval (a named constant, e.g. `MIN_CAPTURE_INTERVAL`,
   not a buried literal). Observable: firing the hook twice within the interval
   yields **one** retained record for the window, not two; firing it after the
   interval yields a second. The rule is a pure, unit-testable predicate over
   the existing history + the new `collectedAt`, separate from I/O.
2. **No same-instant distortion.** The retained series never contains two
   records whose spacing is below the interval, so observed pace
   (`deriveForecast`'s window fold) is computed over genuinely spaced points —
   asserted against a fixture of clustered captures.
3. **Real RAG on captured history.** Given ≥2 retained observations spanning
   ≥ the ADR-0012 minimum span with a committed deadline (or appetite-window),
   the card renders a real `on_track`/`at_risk` band (not `unknown` /
   `insufficient-history`) — asserted end-to-end through the read layer, driven
   by captured (hook-written) records, not only backfilled ones.
4. **Backfill + capture compose.** The spacing rule treats git-backfilled seed
   records (013-01) and session-stop captures as one series (same directory,
   same `readObservationHistory` read) without double-counting or ordering
   errors. Observable: a backfilled seed followed by a fresh capture yields a
   correctly ordered, correctly spaced series.
5. **Honest below the gate.** A project still short of the minimum-history bar
   continues to read explicit `unknown (insufficient-history)` and still shows
   the 013-shipped first-run hint — the spacing rule never fabricates a band it
   has not earned.

**Edge cases to cover explicitly:** exactly-at-interval boundary (inclusive vs.
exclusive, documented); a capture whose `collectedAt` is *older* than the latest
record (clock skew — never reorders/dedups incorrectly); an empty history (first
capture always retained); interaction with a same-day backfill seed.
